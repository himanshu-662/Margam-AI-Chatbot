=== CANADA STUDY PERMIT & ADMISSIONS COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE ===
Official Guide for Indian Aspirants Applying to Canadian Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs)

1. DEGREE PATHWAYS & INTAKES:
- Intakes:
  * Fall Intake (Primary): September start. Application deadlines: January to March.
  * Winter Intake: January start. Application deadlines: June to August.
  * Summer/Spring Intake: May start (Limited courses).
- Degree Types:
  * Master's Degrees (University): 2 years. Academic, research, and thesis-driven or professional.
  * Post-Graduate Diplomas (PG Dip / Ontario College): 1 or 2 years. Career-focused, practical.

2. FINANCIAL REQUIREMENTS & GIC (2026 BENCHMARKS):
- Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC):
  * Mandatory living cost benchmark for international students: CAD $20,635 per year (Approx. INR 12.8 Lakhs).
  * Must be deposited into an eligible Canadian financial institution (e.g., CIBC, Scotiabank, ICICI Canada, SBI Canada) prior to visa submission.
  * Disbursed monthly (CAD ~$1,719/month) upon student arrival in Canada to cover accommodation, food, and essentials.
- Tuition Fees:
  * Master's Degrees: CAD $18,000 to $35,000 per year (INR 11 to 21 Lakhs).
  * Post-Graduate Diplomas: CAD $14,000 to $20,000 per year (INR 8.5 to 12.5 Lakhs).
  * 1st year tuition fee must be paid in full to receive the Official Fee Receipt.

3. PAL (PROVINCIAL ATTESTATION LETTER) RULES:
- Post-2024 regulations require most international undergraduate and diploma applicants to obtain a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) from the provincial government via their university.
- Master's and Doctoral students are typically exempt from the national cap and PAL requirement.

4. VISA APPLICATION FEES:
- Study Permit Application Fee: CAD $150.
- Biometrics Fee: CAD $85.
- Upfront Medical Examination: INR 6,000 to 9,000 from IRCC-approved panel physicians.

5. POST-GRADUATION WORK PERMIT (PGWP) & WORK LIMITS:
- Master's graduates: Eligible for a full 3-year PGWP, even for 1-year or 16-month Master's programs.
- Diploma graduates: 2-year programs qualify for up to 3 years PGWP; 1-year programs qualify for a 1-year PGWP.
- Off-Campus Part-Time Work: Up to 24 hours per week during academic semesters, full-time during designated academic breaks.

6. MARGAM ADVISORY TIPS FOR VISA APPROVAL:
- Statement of Purpose (Letter of Explanation): Must demonstrate clear career progression from undergraduate studies. Address why Canada and why this specific DLI over options in India.
- Avoid low-tier non-DLI institutions; prioritize public colleges and recognized universities with high employment track records.
