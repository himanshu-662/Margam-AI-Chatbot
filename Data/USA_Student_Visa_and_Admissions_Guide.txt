=== USA F-1 STUDENT VISA & ADMISSIONS COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE ===
Official Guide for Indian Aspirants Applying to US Universities

1. DEGREE STRUCTURE & INTAKES:
- Intakes: 
  * Fall Intake (Primary): August/September start. Application deadlines: November to February.
  * Spring Intake (Secondary): January start. Application deadlines: July to October.
- Degree Duration: Master's (MS/MBA): 1.5 to 2 years (30–36 credits). Bachelor's (BS/BA): 4 years.
- Standardized Testing:
  * GRE: Typically recommended or required for competitive STEM MS programs (Target: 310+ with 160+ Quant).
  * English Proficiency: TOEFL (80–100+), IELTS (6.5–7.5), or Duolingo English Test (DET 115–130).

2. TUITION FEES & FINANCIAL REQUIREMENTS (I-20):
- Average Annual Tuition:
  * Public State Universities: $22,000 to $35,000 per year (INR 18.5 to 29.5 Lakhs).
  * Private Universities: $38,000 to $60,000 per year (INR 32 to 50 Lakhs).
- Estimated Annual Living Expenses: $12,000 to $18,000 per year (INR 10 to 15 Lakhs).
- I-20 Document Proof of Funds:
  * The University issues Form I-20 only after verifying liquid funds covering 1 full academic year (Tuition + Living + Health Insurance).
  * Typically required liquid balance: $35,000 to $65,000 (INR 30 to 55 Lakhs) shown via bank savings, fixed deposits, or sanctioned education loan letters.

3. MANDATORY VISA FEES (2026 RATES):
- SEVIS I-901 Fee: $350 (Paid online on fmjfee.com before booking visa appointment).
- DS-160 Visa Application Fee (MRV): $185 (Paid to US Embassy/Consulate).
- No mandatory blocked account; financial capability is proven via liquid bank balance and sanctioned education loans.

4. POST-STUDY WORK RIGHTS (OPT & STEM EXTENSION):
- Optional Practical Training (OPT): 12 months for all F-1 degree graduates.
- STEM OPT Extension: 24 additional months for designated Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math programs.
- Total Work Authorization: 36 months (3 full years) on F-1 visa without needing H-1B sponsorship immediately.
- CPT (Curricular Practical Training): Available during course for accredited internships.

5. MARGAM ADVISORY TIPS FOR VISA SUCCESS:
- Section 214(b) Non-Immigrant Intent: The visa officer evaluates your economic and social ties to India. Never express intent to settle permanently during the consular interview.
- Clarify career ROI: Articulate how your US degree bridges to senior high-paying roles in multinational companies in India and globally.
