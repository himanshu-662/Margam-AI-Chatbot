=== AUSTRALIA SUBCLASS 500 STUDENT VISA & ADMISSIONS GUIDE ===
Official Guide for Indian Aspirants Applying to Australian Universities

1. DEGREE STRUCTURE & INTAKES:
- Intakes:
  * Semester 1 (Primary Intake): February / March start. Application deadlines: October to December.
  * Semester 2: July / August start. Application deadlines: March to May.
  * Trimester 3 / Summer: October / November (Limited universities).
- Degree Types:
  * Master's by Coursework: 1.5 to 2 years.
  * Master's by Research: 2 years.
  * Bachelor's Degrees: 3 to 4 years.
- Group of Eight (Go8): Top-tier research universities (Univ of Melbourne, Sydney, UNSW, ANU, Monash, Queensland, UWA, Adelaide).

2. FINANCIAL EVIDENCE & LIVING COST REQUIREMENTS:
- Department of Home Affairs Financial Capacity Requirement:
  * Mandatory living cost benchmark: AUD $29,710 per year (Approx. INR 16.3 Lakhs).
  * In addition to living costs, applicants must demonstrate proof of 1st year tuition fee plus return airfare (AUD ~$2,000).
  * Funds must be demonstrable via liquid savings accounts, fixed deposits (held for minimum 3 months), or recognized bank education loans.
- Average Tuition Fees:
  * Group of Eight Universities: AUD $38,000 to $52,000 per year (INR 21 to 28.5 Lakhs).
  * Other Public Universities: AUD $28,000 to $36,000 per year (INR 15.5 to 20 Lakhs).

3. MANDATORY HEALTH INSURANCE & VISA FEES:
- Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC):
  * Mandatory health insurance for entire visa duration.
  * Single coverage cost: AUD $600 to $850 per year (Providers: Bupa, Allianz Care, Medibank).
- Visa Application Charge (Subclass 500): AUD $710 (Paid to Department of Home Affairs).

4. GENUINE STUDENT (GS) REQUIREMENT:
- Replaced the older GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) standard.
- Assesses student circumstances: academic background, reasons for selecting course and Australia, financial stability, and how the qualification empowers career advancement upon completion.

5. POST-STUDY WORK RIGHTS (SUBCLASS 485):
- Bachelor's Graduates: 2 years post-study work visa.
- Master's Graduates (Coursework & Research): 2 to 3 years.
- Regional Area Study Bonus: 1 to 2 additional years for studying outside major metropolitan centers (e.g., Adelaide, Perth, Gold Coast, Wollongong).
- Work limits during study: 48 hours per fortnight during study terms, unrestricted during scheduled semester breaks.

6. MARGAM ADVISORY TIPS:
- Ensure Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) is issued by the university before submitting visa.
- Keep financial sponsor relationships close (self, parents, siblings) with formal income tax returns (ITR) from the last 2-3 financial years.
